YouTube Audio Downloader

Download your favorite YouTube videos as MP3 files

How to Download

1

Find Your Video

Go to YouTube and find the video you want to download as audio.

2

Copy the URL

Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar.

3

Paste and Download

Paste the URL into the input field above and click the Download button.

4

Find Your Audio File

Your audio file will be automatically downloaded to the 'downloads' folder in your project directory. The file will be saved as an MP3 with the video's title as the filename.
